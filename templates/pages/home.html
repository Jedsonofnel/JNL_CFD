Project summary

A lightweight velocity-pressure solver built using golang and compiled to WebAssembly (WASM), designed around ease of use and transparency.

Domain language

I am heavily influenced by the work, writings and general musings of DHH and the team at 37signals, likely due to my time spent working with Rails and subsequent exposure to that community. I really enjoy their domain driven boldness and the usage of creative domain language to add a bit of personality while also making the underlying ideas concrete. Imperfectly, I am being intentional about incorporating similarly meaningful domain language to this project, which aligns with the goal of removing any magic or obscure jargon.

The following are some key domain ideas and names:

  • Peek: A mesh or a case made visual. My ideal is to have as much be Peekable as possible, and the term serves to describe the goal of seeing what is under the hood.
